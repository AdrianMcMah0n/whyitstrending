Why It's Trending

The story behind whatever the internet is talking about today, in under a minute.

Why It's Trending is a short-form video channel. Each video takes one trending topic, gathers what is actually being reported on it, and explains the context in about 45 seconds.

How the videos are made

Topics come from publicly available trending feeds. For each topic, the published news coverage is collected and summarised into a short narrated script. Every factual claim is credited to the outlet that reported it, both on screen while it is spoken and in the description under each video.

Summaries are assembled from published reporting with the help of automated tools. Background footage is generic stock and does not depict the actual events described. Facts are as reported at the time of publication and may change as a story develops.

About this site

This page exists to describe the channel and the small private application used to publish to it. That application is used only by the channel owner, to upload videos to the channel's own YouTube account. It is not offered to other people and collects nothing from visitors to this page.
